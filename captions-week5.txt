=== 2026-11-17 21:00 KST — 마진 vs 마크업(50% 마크업=33% 마진)
A 50% markup is only a 33% margin 💡

Margin = profit ÷ selling price
Markup = profit ÷ cost

Example: cost $12, price $25 gives a 52% margin but a 108.33% markup.

The Margin & markup calculator at calc.315tools.com works it out from cost and price, a target margin or a markup.

Link in bio 👆
